Rental System

Don't have enough money to buy your own property yet? But want a place to safely store your items? With our custom rental system, players can rent apartments, plots, and other properties throughout the world. New players are homeless by default and will have a [Homeless] prefix in the chat. Once you rent/buy a property or plot the [Homeless] prefix will disappear and your income tax will go from 45% to 35%. More info on taxes here.

How it works​

When you approach a property that is available for rent, a BossBar will appear at the top of your screen showing the rental price and lease duration. To rent a property or plot, type /q rent (for property) or /plot rent (for plot).

Secure rentals​

Once you rent a property, it becomes protected for the duration of your lease. The landlord cannot access your chests, break your blocks, or interfere with your belongings while you are renting.

Embassies​

Players can rent apartments in foreign towns while remaining part of their current town, allowing them to live and work across different regions without changing towns.

Renting out​

To rent out a quarter or plot that you own, stand in it and type /q forrent [days] [amount] (for quarters) or /plot forrent [days] [amount] (for plots). To make a quarter or plot unavailable for rent, stand in it and type /q notforrent or /plot notforrent. To evict a renter, stand in it and type /q unrent or /plot unrent.

Commands​

CommandDescription
/q rentRent available property
/plot rentRent available plot
/q forrent [days] [amount]Set property for rent
/plot forrent [days] [amount]Set plot for rent
/q notforrentMake property unavailable for rent
/plot notforrentMake plot unavailable for rent
/q unrentEvict renter from property
/plot unrentEvict renter from plot
