:::info[Info]

Welcome to the rules page! Server rules are needed to ensure a fun and fair gameplay experience for everyone. Feel free to contact staff if you have any questions about the rules.

:::

## 1. Cheating, server exploits and alternative accounts

### 1.1 Cheat clients and other modifications

Cheat clients, macro keys and other modifications which give the player gameplay leverage over other players are not allowed. Playing using stolen or illegitimately obtained accounts is also not allowed. Players found breaking this rule will face a permanent ban.

**This rule does not apply to:**

- OptiFine and other performance improvement mods.
- Armor and effect status HUD mods.
- Minimap mods unless they display more information than our website map provides.
- Purely aesthetic mods.
- Litematica without printer function (Schematica is not allowed however).

Please contact server staff if you are unsure about whether a client modification you are using is allowed.

### 1.2 Knowingly benefiting from or assisting others in breaking server rules

Players found intentionally assisting in, knowingly participating in, or knowingly benefitting from other players breaking any of the rules listed on this page may be treated as if they committed the act themselves.

### 1.3 Technical errors and server exploits

Players are not allowed to take advantage of apparent server bugs or otherwise exploit technical oversights. Players found taking advantage of bugs or technical oversights without notifying staff may be permanently banned.

### 1.4 Alternative accounts

Alternative accounts or “alts” for short are not allowed. You may only use one account per player - account sharing isn’t allowed. Alternative accounts will be banned and the player’s main account may be warned if this rule is deliberately broken. A second instance of violating this rule would result in the player's main account being banned as well. Players are permitted to play from the same connection as long as they are the ones solely operating their account.

Please contact server staff if you wish to switch your main account.

### 1.5 Intentionally causing lag or disrupting NationMC services

Players intentionally causing harm to the services we provide may be permanently banned.

## 2. Behavior and chat

### 2.1 Naming

#### Nation names

Nation names must be named after a current or past non-fictional nation, region, province or other large geographical area with defined borders.

In-game location of the nation capital should also correspond with nation/region/province's real life borders.

#### Town names

Town names cannot be inappropriate, toxic, or offensive.

If a town's name corresponds to a real-world location, the town created closest to that location has priority. Admins handle such cases and may rename misplaced towns.

#### Player names

Account usernames, nicknames, skins and Towny titles must not be advertisements, explicit, sexual or otherwise inappropriate.

### 2.2 Stealing and raiding players/towns/nations

Raiding and stealing from other players is allowed. It’s up to each and every player to protect their assets.

### 2.3 Spam

Spamming or sending messages in a way that disrupts chat flow, normally by sending a similar message repeatedly, is not allowed.

### 2.4 Unwanted behavior

Listed unwanted behavior is not allowed on the server. Unwanted behavior includes:

- Any form of explicit bullying or derogatory language.
- Comments that encourage self-harm or incite violence, including hate speech against racial and social minorities.
- Explicit discussions or advances of a sexual nature are prohibited.
- Unsubstantiated or harmful statements about a player’s character that could damage their “real life” reputation.
- Any interaction that involves grooming, coercing, or attempting to form inappropriate relationships with players, especially minors, is strictly prohibited both on and off the server. This includes, but is not limited to, asking for personal information, inappropriate messages, flirting, or engaging in conversations that exploit or manipulate younger players.

Toxicity is evaluated on a case by case basis and staff has final say in what is considered toxic behavior. Repeated behavior may lead to mutes, warnings, and eventually a permanent ban.

### 2.5 Impersonating other players, towns and nations

Impersonating players by taking advantage of, but not limited to, usernames, nicknames, town/nation names is not allowed. Offenders will be asked to revert to their proper character. If any damage is done staff may choose to ban the offending player.

### 2.7 Sharing personal information

Sharing personal information and identities of others without their explicit permission is strictly forbidden. Personal information may be, but not limited to, full names, “real life” photos of players, phone numbers, home addresses, social networks containing personal information, school/work location, family relations and IP addresses. This includes information disclosed by others themselves. A player sharing their personal information to you does not authorize you to disclose it to other parties on their behalf.

It is also not allowed to leverage others personal information in a threatening or manipulative way.

### 2.8 Sharing links

Malicious links are not allowed, this includes for instance viruses or IP loggers. Links which contribute to breaking any of the rules listed on this page are not allowed. This includes sending links to other players in Discord for instance as well.

Linking to sexual or extremely violent/disturbing content is not allowed. Other links are allowed, although they may get caught by the spam filter.

### 2.9 Advertising

Advertising is not allowed unless it’s related to the server.

### 2.10 Selling content and services for real money

You are not allowed to sell in-game items or services for real life money, or *vice versa*.

### 2.11 Language in global chat channels

When speaking in the global chat channels you should stick to English only. You can use other private chat channels for other languages such as town chat or private messages. If there are only or almost only players who also speak your language online you are allowed to speak it in the global chat channels too.

## 3. Griefing

### 3.1 Destroying land in or around claims

Players may not damage land in or 5 chunks around claims (griefing). Players breaking this rule will be warned and damage will be rolled back. Players doing this more than once may face a permanent ban.

**Examples of grief (but not limited to):**

- Cobble monsters – Mountain-like structures that are built using lava and water.
- Lava/water curtains – Placing rows of lava/water in the sky to make a wall of falling lava.
- TNT craters.
- Mass spawning entities within or around a town (chickens, boats, etc.).

Towns that are created near other towns for the purpose of intentionally disrupting neighboring town's experience (Grief inside own claims) is also prohibited.

### 3.2 Destroying land in wilderness

Wilderness is defined by Towny as any land not currently claimed. You are allowed to grief structures or terrain as long as they are not within or in close proximity to a claimed area. However, any player may request major terrain grief to be rolled back even in the wilderness.

Breaking railways or ice roads is not allowed even in the wilderness, if willingly connecting two existing towns. This does not only apply to the rails but all blocks the railroad is made of.

### 3.3 Town claims

Towns claiming in a way to restrict the ability of neighboring towns to expand, referred to as claim blocking, is not allowed. Claim blocking can be in a form of claim arm or intent to claim block.

Creating claim arms extending from towns is not allowed. These are protrusions which are often used to block the settlement and expansion of other towns or just artificially expand the area a town controls without the need to claim all of it. Exceptions are granted to ships. Claim arms created as a result of ocean outposts are allowed as long as the arm is unclaimed later.

Towns are not allowed to have areas of unclaimed chunks inside their borders, as this can substantially increase the size of a town without them actually having to increase their claim limit legitimately.

Towns are also not allowed to overclaim another town's chunks in a way which would split the overclaimed town into two segments. Any overclaim of chunks must leave the overclaimed town as a solid contiguous whole.

### 3.4 Stealing specific blocks

Stealing specific blocks for their value is not considered grief and is therefore allowed. This does not apply to easily acquired blocks even if they are valuable to you.

### 3.5 Major terraforming

In order to preserve the accurate geography of the map, major terraforming of the natural terrain is not allowed. In general, if your edits of the landscape alter the website map view of the natural landscape, they will be removed. Examples of this include for instance draining large lakes/rivers, creating artificial islands which do not exist in real life, or covering the ocean with platforms. Vertical terraforming is allowed as it does not change the website map view.

### 3.6 Map art

Creating map art is allowed as long as it does not interfere with other towns or cause major terraforming (for example, over oceans). Exceptions can be made to map platforms over oceans that are made of glass and are used regularly. It is recommended to place map art in Greenland as there is a lot of unused space there. If you place it outside of Greenland you will have to claim the area as it will count as proximity griefing if the other towns around you don’t like it.

Your map art may not contain nudity, sexual content, racist imagery or anything else that breaks any part of rule section 2.
