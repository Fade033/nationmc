The economy is the heartbeat of NationMC. We use [chest shops](/docs/Shops) for player-run shops, paired with our custom **NationMC Treasury & Tax Engine**. Taxes are here to battle inflation and for many more reasons (which we will get into below).

## Money
Money can be used to buy items from shops, [permissions](/docs/Shops#license), plots, quarters, casino chips and crate keys, etc.

## Property tax
To prevent abandoned towns and properties from cluttering the map, **all property owners must pay daily property taxes**.

How it works:

*   Every 24 hours, a small tax is automatically deducted from your balance based on how many plots you own and/or how many blocks your apartments contain in total. To see how much you need to pay and to pay in advance, run the `/nmc` command. If you cannot pay your taxes, the server will **proportionally foreclose** your property. *Note: You will only lose the specific plot or apartment you couldn't pay for. Your town and mayor status are completely safe!* 

Tax rate:

*   Plot (chunk): **$150** / day
*   Apartment: **$0.48** / 100 blocks / day

*Note: If you rent a plot or apartment, you dont have to pay any property taxes for that plot or apartment. Only the owner does!*

:::warning[Warning]

After getting your first plot or apartment, make sure to have money available to pay the property tax. Otherwise you will immediately lose your plot or apartment.

:::
:::info[Info]

Make sure to check every day how much property tax you need to pay by typing `/nmc`. Or you can pay your taxes in advance so that you can check less often and avoid risking losing your property.

:::

## Income tax

Income tax is here to help with developing towns and nations, incentives and emergencies. Tax funds are collected from each chest shop sale (35%) and stored into the treasury. If you do **not** rent/own at least 1 [quarter](/docs/Quarters) or [plot](/docs/Town#plots), then your income tax is set to 45% and you will have a [Homeless] prefix in chat until you rent/own a quarter or plot.

## How to earn money

### Mining
Earn money by mining resources in the wild and selling them at `/spawn`. You can teleport to the wild at spawn.

### Shops

[Open a shop](/docs/Shops) and sell items to other players. The only thing you need is sufficient stock of the item you are trying to sell, competitive pricing, and a good location. Left-click a chest with the item you want to sell and follow the instructions. The most successful shops are built in [nation](/docs/Nation) capitals, nation capitals have the benefit of attracting a lot of customers through the nation spawn. The shopping districts of capitals are usually one of the main sources of income for nations and players alike. To learn more about shops, check out the [shops](/docs/Shops) page.

### Real estate

You can also earn money by buying real estate/plots and renting them out or selling them for more.
