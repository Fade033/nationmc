Don't have enough money to buy your own property yet? With our custom rental system, players can rent [apartments](/docs/Quarters), [plots](/docs/Town#plots), and other properties throughout the world. New players are homeless by default and will have a [Homeless] prefix in the chat. Once you rent/buy a property or plot the [Homeless] prefix will disappear and your [income tax](/docs/Economy#income-tax) will go from 45% to 35%. More info on taxes [here](/docs/Economy).

## How it works
When you approach a property that is available for rent, a **BossBar** will appear at the top of your screen showing the rental price and lease duration. To rent a property or plot, type `/q rent` (for property) or `/plot rent` (for plot).

### Secure rentals
Once you rent a property, it becomes protected for the duration of your lease. The landlord cannot access your chests, break your blocks, or interfere with your belongings while you are renting.

### Embassies
Players can rent apartments in foreign towns while remaining part of their current town, allowing them to live and work across different regions without changing towns.

## Renting out

To rent out a [quarter](/docs/Quarters) or [plot](/docs/Town#plots) that you own, stand in it and type `/q forrent [days] [amount]` (for quarters) or `/plot forrent [days] [amount]` (for plots). To make a quarter or plot unavailable for rent, stand in it and type `/q notforrent` or `/plot notforrent`. To evict a renter, stand in it and type `/q unrent` or `/plot unrent`.
