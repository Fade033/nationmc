You can teleport to your own town, towns within your nation, capitals of allied nations, and your bed.

- `/t spawn`
- `/t spawn <town>`
- `/t warp <warp>`
- `/t warp <town> <warp>`
- `/n spawn`
- `/n spawn <nation>`
- `/home`

Player-to-player teleportation is **not** supported.

This system ensures that your nation's capital becomes a central hub, just as capitals are in real life. Capitals naturally become trade centers because nation residents and residents of allied nations can teleport there directly.

If you're new to the server, start by joining or creating a [town](/docs/Town). If you've just created your town, you won't immediately be able to teleport to other nations. First, join a nearby [nation](/docs/Nation) using `/n join <nation>`.

Most existing towns are already part of a nation.

Once you're part of a nation:

- Use `/t spawn` to teleport to your town spawn.
- Use `/n spawn` to teleport to your nation's capital.
- Use `/n spawn <nation>` to teleport to the capital of an allied nation.

## Warps

Warps are used to explore different parts of the city since the city is huge. To open the warp menu, type `/pw`.
