# FAQ

Short answers to the questions players ask most often about Panic!. For details, follow the links to the other pages of this section.

### Do I need to install anything?
No. Panic! runs entirely on the server. Any client that follows the server rules works as before.

### Does Panic! get better over time?
Yes. Panic! learns what normal play looks like on NationMC and on all partner servers, and it learns from confirmed reports. The longer it runs, the more precisely it tells skill from cheating. See [Overview](./overview).

### Can I be banned because of lag or a bad connection?
No. Panic! evaluates patterns over time and is built to tolerate lag and high ping. A single unusual moment is never treated as cheating.

### I reported a cheater, but they are still online. Did my report work?
Yes. Panic! observes before it acts, and bans may be issued in waves rather than immediately. You do not need to report again. See [Reports & Fair Play](./reports).

### Does the player I reported know who reported them?
No. Reports are confidential and are visible only to staff and to Panic!.

### I was in the queue and now I am at the end again. Why?
A slot opened for you, but it was not claimed within five minutes. Reconnect around your estimated waiting time to avoid missing your slot. See [Queue System](./queue).

### Can I buy a place in the queue?
No. Panic! does not sell priority. Priority in the Panic! queue is earned by leaving voluntarily with `/panic leave` while others are waiting. It is not connected to ranks or the store.

### Why was I asked to leave the server?
Someone had been waiting for a long time and you had the longest session. This is not a punishment. You keep all progress and receive queue priority as compensation. See [Queue System](./queue).

### Is Panic! only on NationMC?
No. Panic! protects several partner servers, which share one ban list and the same protection standard.

### What do I do if I think a ban was wrong?
Contact the NationMC staff on [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/f6h98zac5a) with your in-game name and the approximate time of the ban. See [Reports & Fair Play](./reports).
