Reports & Fair Play

Protected by Panic!

If you believe a player is cheating, you can report them with a single command. Panic! evaluates every report and decides whether the reported player should be observed more closely. This page explains how to report and what happens afterwards.

In short
  • Report with /report <player> [reason]. The reported player does not see your report.
  • Panic! weighs your report and may place the player under closer observation.
  • Confirmed reports raise your credibility; unfounded reports lower it.

How to report​

Use /report <player> [reason], for example /report Steve killaura. The reason is optional, but it helps.

When you press Tab, Panic! suggests common reasons such as cheating, killaura, speed, xray and autoclick. You can only report players who are currently online, and you cannot report yourself or members of staff.

Reports are confidential. The reported player is not notified, and your name is visible only to staff and to Panic!.

What happens after a report​

  1. Panic! weighs the report. It considers your reporter credibility, reports from other players about the same person, and what it already knows about that player's behavior.
  2. The player may be placed under observation. If the combined picture is plausible, Panic! watches the reported player more closely for a limited time. You receive a short confirmation telling you whether the report was registered or observation is active.
  3. Panic! decides. If the observation confirms cheating, Panic! acts and your report counts as confirmed. If nothing is found, the observation ends automatically and the player is not affected in any way.
One report is enough

Panic! combines reports from different players automatically. Repeating a report does not speed anything up.

Reporter credibility​

Every player has a reporter credibility that starts at a neutral level. Confirmed reports raise it; reports that turn out to be unfounded lower it. Reports from players with high credibility weigh more and trigger observation faster.

If your credibility drops too far, your reports are no longer counted until it recovers. Keep it high by reporting only what you have actually seen.

Rules for reporting​

  • Report only players you genuinely suspect. Being better than you is not a reason.
  • Do not report as a joke or to annoy someone. Deliberate false reports lower your credibility and may be treated as a rules violation by staff.
  • Short waiting periods apply between reports and before you can report the same player again. This is normal and not an error.
  • Cheating itself is forbidden under Rule 1.1.
False reports have consequences

Panic! remembers which reports were justified. Repeated unfounded reports lower your credibility until your reports are no longer counted.

If you believe Panic! made a mistake​

Panic! is built to avoid wrong decisions, but no system is perfect. If you were banned and believe this is a mistake, contact the NationMC staff on Discord with your in-game name and the approximate time of the ban.

Staff can review the case together with Panic!. Do not create a new account to get around a ban: alternative accounts are not allowed under Rule 1.4, and ban evasion makes a review harder.
