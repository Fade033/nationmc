NationMC takes place on a breathtaking **1:500 scale Earth map**. At the heart of this world lies the biggest, most immersive, and realistic city ever built in Minecraft, featuring completely custom textures and awe-inspiring architecture. 

## Main menu
This is where you can find the most important features to navigate the server. You can open the menu with `/menu`.

## [Map ↗](https://map.nationmc.org)

After joining the server, the first thing you need to do is find out where you spawned. Type `/map` and get a clickable map link in chat. You can also visit the default map by clicking the link [map.nationmc.org](https://map.nationmc.org/). Scroll to zoom in.

The map shows:

*   All online players
*   Changes made to the terrain
*   Claimed land
*   Shops
*   Server spawn (type `/spawn` to go to spawn)

Colored squares on the map represent claimed Towny land. You can click on them to get more information about the town.

## Surviving

In order to survive you will need to mine resources in the wilderniss (wilderniss teleport is at spawn) and sell them at spawn by running the `/spawn` command.
