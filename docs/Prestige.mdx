In the cutthroat, geopolitical world of NationMC, wealth isn’t just about what is sitting inside your vault, it’s about what you show the world. The **Prestige** system is your ultimate way to flex your power and command respect. Climb the social ladder from a humble citizen to a tier **X** Elite and let your chat presence do the talking. Once you buy a **Prestige** tier, you own it forever. Even if you lose your town, go bankrupt, or get the [Homeless] tag, your **Prestige** prefix will remain attached to your name. A tier **X** will stay a legend forever!

## Why prestige?

#### The benefits:

- **Exclusive Chat Prefixes:** Every time you speak in the chat, your **Prestige** tier is displayed next to your name. Stand out from the crowd with tags that standard players cannot get.
- **Leverage:** In a server driven by politics and wars, appearance is everything. When a player with a [**`[Prestige VIII]`**](/docs/Prestige#why-prestige) tag enters a negotiation or declares war, people listen. It shows you have the resources to fund empires and armies.
- **The Ultimate Flex:** Anyone can claim to be rich, but only the true powerful players can actually show it. It is a permanent badge of honor that proves your dominance over the server's economy.
- **The Rags-to-Riches Story:** There is no greater feeling on NationMC than starting with the [Homeless] tag, building a business empire, and eventually earning the **last Prestige** level.

## How to prestige

Prestiging is really simple:

- Type `/nmc` to open the [economy](/docs/Economy) menu.
- Locate the **Nether Star** and click on it to **Prestige** to the next level.
