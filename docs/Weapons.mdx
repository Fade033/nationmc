NationMC isn't just about building; it's about warfare and survival. We have heavily modified the vanilla experience to include deep RPG mechanics. We use custom firearms, tactical reloads, unique melee weapons, and advanced combat physics. Prepare for intense, skill-based PvP!

The server also features lots of custom items, unique building blocks, custom furniture, and specialized armor sets that you won't find in vanilla Minecraft.

## How to buy weapons

Type `/pw`, [teleport](/docs/Teleporting#warps) to the Greenfield Market and go to the gun store to buy yourself a weapon. To use a weapon, hold `right-click` to shoot and `left-click` to aim.
