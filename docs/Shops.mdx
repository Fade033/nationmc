## Introduction

Creating a shop is a great way to [earn money](/docs/Economy#how-to-earn-money) and plays a central role in the server's economy. Players run the markets.

## License
Before you can create a chest shop, you need to buy a Business License at `/spawn`.

*Note: You don't need a Business License to buy from player shops and to sell to player shops.*

## Shop plot

Shops can only be made in shop plots. Buy a plot in a town with command `/plot claim`. Mayors do not need to buy plots, they have access to all plots by default. Change [plot type](/docs/Town#plots) from default to shop with `/plot set shop`.

## Create the shop

Once that's over with you can start creating the shop.

Place a chest inside the shop plot.
- Hold the item you wish to sell.
- Left click the chest while holding the item.
- Enter in chat how much money you want to sell the item for.
- Place stock in chest.

You have now successfully created a shop!

## Good to know

Remember to fill the chest with items to sell. Red sign text indicates that stock has run out. Consider creating a double chest shop for larger stock storage.

Clicking a shop with higher item quanity will sell that quantity for the specififed amount of money. For example, you will sell a bulk of 64 planks if you click a shop holding 64 planks.

## Shop commands

Look at shop you want to manage with below commands.

      <td>/qs sell</td>
      <td>Change the shop to sell mode.</td>

    <tr>
      <td>/qs price [price]</td>
      <td>Set the shop price.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>/qs size</td>

    </tr>
    <tr>

      <td>Add a player as shop staff.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>/qs staff remove [player]</td>

    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>/qs staff clear</td>
      <td>Remove all staff members from the shop.</td>
